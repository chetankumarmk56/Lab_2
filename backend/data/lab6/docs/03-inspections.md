# Inspection Scheduling & Results Policy

## When Inspections Are Required

Permitted work must be inspected at each stage listed on the permit before it
is concealed or built over. Typical stages are: foundation/footing, framing,
rough-in (electrical, plumbing, mechanical), insulation, and final. Work that
is covered before inspection may be ordered exposed at the owner's expense.

## Scheduling an Inspection

Inspections are requested through the permit portal or by phone using the
BP application number. Requests must be made at least 48 hours before the
requested date. Inspections are performed Monday through Friday in two arrival
windows: morning (8:00 AM – 12:00 PM) and afternoon (12:30 PM – 4:00 PM). The
assigned window is confirmed by 5:00 PM the day before the inspection.

## Preparing for an Inspection

The permit card and approved plans must be on site and accessible. Work must be
complete for the stage being inspected, safely accessible, and adequately lit.
An adult (18 or older) must provide access if the site is occupied or locked.

## Inspection Results

Each inspection is recorded as one of:

- **Pass** — the stage is approved and work may proceed.
- **Partial** — a defined portion is approved; the remainder must be
  reinspected.
- **Fail** — deficiencies were found. The inspector leaves a written correction
  notice listing each deficiency and the code reference.

## Reinspections

Failed or not-ready inspections require a reinspection, which is subject to the
reinspection fee in the Permit Fees & Payment Policy. Corrections must be
completed and the reinspection requested within 10 business days of the failed
inspection, unless the inspector notes a longer correction period on the
notice.

## Final Inspection and Certificate of Completion

A final inspection is required for every permit. When the final inspection
passes, the Certificate of Completion (or Certificate of Occupancy for new
buildings) is issued within 3 business days. A building may not be occupied
before its Certificate of Occupancy is issued.
