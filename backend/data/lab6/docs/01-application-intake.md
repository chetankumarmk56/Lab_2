# Permit Application & Intake Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes how Riverbend County accepts and screens building permit
applications. It applies to all residential and commercial construction permits
issued by the Building Services Division.

## How to Apply

Applications are accepted through the online permit portal (available 24/7) or
in person at the Building Services counter, Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to
4:30 PM. Applications submitted by mail are not accepted. Each application must
be submitted by the property owner or a contractor authorized in writing by the
owner.

## Required Documents

Every application must include:

- A completed application form signed by the owner or authorized agent.
- A site plan showing property lines, existing structures, and the proposed work.
- Construction drawings for any structural, electrical, or plumbing work.
- A contractor license number, or an owner-builder affidavit where permitted.
- Payment of the base permit fee (see the Permit Fees & Payment Policy).

Incomplete submissions are logged but are not considered "received" until every
required document is on file.

## Completeness Review

Building Services performs a completeness review within 5 business days of
submission. If the application is incomplete, the applicant receives a written
deficiency notice listing the missing items. The applicant has 30 calendar days
to supply the missing items; otherwise the application is closed and a new
application is required.

## Application Numbers

Accepted applications are assigned a tracking number in the format
BP-YYYY-NNNNN (for example, BP-2026-00341). This number must be quoted on all
correspondence, inspection requests, and appeals.

## Processing Targets

Once an application is complete, plan review targets are 15 business days for
standard residential work and 30 business days for commercial work. Complex
projects (over three stories, or over 20,000 square feet) may take longer; the
assigned plan reviewer will provide a schedule in writing.
