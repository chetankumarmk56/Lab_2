# Records Retention & Public Access Policy

## Public Records Requests

Permit records are public records. Copies may be requested with Form PR-1
online or at the counter. The County responds within 10 business days with the
records, a schedule for producing them, or the legal basis for any denial.
Standard copies are $0.25 per page; electronic copies of records already held
digitally are free.

## What Is Public and What Is Redacted

Permit applications, issued permits, inspection results, and Certificates of
Occupancy are public. The County redacts: interior floor plans of occupied
residences (safety), security system details, and personal contact information
other than the name of the license holder of record. Trade-secret material in
commercial plans may be withheld if it was marked at submission.

## Retention Schedule

- Permit files (application, permit, inspection record, certificate): retained
  7 years after final action on the permit.
- Approved construction plans: retained 2 years after the Certificate of
  Occupancy or Completion is issued.
- Records of unpermitted-work enforcement: retained 10 years after the case is
  closed.

Records past their retention date are destroyed on an annual cycle authorized
by the County Records Committee.

## Corrections

A property owner may request correction of a factual error in a permit record
(for example, a wrong address or misspelled name) by written request with
supporting documentation. Corrections are logged as amendments; the original
entry is preserved with a note, not erased.
