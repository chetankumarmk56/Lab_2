# Permit Expiration & Renewal Policy

## Permit Validity

A building permit is valid for 180 days from the date of issuance. Work must
begin within 90 days of issuance; a permit under which no work has begun after
90 days is void and a new application is required.

## Keeping a Permit Active

A permit remains active as long as the work passes at least one required
inspection every 180 days. If more than 180 days pass without a passing
inspection, the permit expires automatically — no separate notice is issued.

## Extensions

The permit holder may request one extension of 90 days. The request must be
made in writing at least 14 days before the expiration date, must describe the
work remaining, and is subject to a $45 extension fee. Extensions are granted
at the discretion of the Building Official and are not available for permits
that are already expired.

## Renewing an Expired Permit

An expired permit may be renewed within 60 days of expiration for 50% of the
original permit fee, provided the approved plans and code edition are
unchanged. After 60 days, renewal is not available: a new application, new plan
review, and full fees are required, and the work will be reviewed under the
code in effect at the time of the new application.

## Suspended or Revoked Permits

The Building Official may suspend or revoke a permit issued in error, or where
work materially deviates from the approved plans. Work must stop immediately
upon written notice. A revoked permit is not eligible for renewal or extension;
the holder may appeal the revocation under the Appeals & Dispute Resolution
Policy.
