# Appeals & Dispute Resolution Policy

## What May Be Appealed

An applicant or permit holder may appeal: a permit denial, a stop-work order, a
permit revocation or suspension, a failed inspection's correction notice, or a
fee determination. Field interpretations of the building code may also be
appealed where an equivalent alternative method of construction is proposed.

## How to File an Appeal

Appeals are filed on Form AP-2 with the Clerk of the Board of Appeals. The
deadline is 14 calendar days from the date printed on the decision being
appealed. A $60 filing fee applies; the fee is refunded in full if the appeal
is upheld. Filing an appeal does not lift a stop-work order — work remains
stopped while the appeal is pending unless the Building Official grants a
written exception.

## The Board of Appeals

The Board of Appeals has five members appointed by the Board of Supervisors,
including at least one licensed architect, one licensed engineer, and one
licensed general contractor. Three members constitute a quorum.

## Hearing Timeline

The Board schedules a hearing within 45 calendar days of a complete filing.
The appellant and the Building Official each present evidence; hearings are
public. The Board issues a written decision within 10 business days of the
hearing. The decision may uphold, reverse, or modify the original
determination.

## Further Review

A party dissatisfied with the Board's decision may seek judicial review in
superior court within 30 calendar days of the written decision. No further
administrative review is available within the County.
