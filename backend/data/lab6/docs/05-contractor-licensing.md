# Contractor Licensing Requirements

## Who Must Be Licensed

Any person performing construction work valued over $1,000, or any electrical,
plumbing, or mechanical work regardless of value, must hold a current Riverbend
County contractor license or a state license recognized by reciprocity. Permits
are only issued to licensed contractors or qualifying owner-builders.

## License Classes

- **Class A — General Engineering/Commercial:** all building types, unlimited
  value.
- **Class B — General Residential:** one- and two-family dwellings and their
  accessory structures.
- **Class C — Specialty:** a single trade, such as C-10 Electrical, C-36
  Plumbing, or C-20 HVAC.

## Insurance and Bonding

Licensed contractors must keep on file with the County:

- General liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence.
- Workers' compensation coverage for all employees, as required by state law.
- A license bond of $25,000 for Class A and Class B, or $10,000 for Class C.

A permit is not issued if any required insurance or bond has lapsed.

## Owner-Builder Exemption

An owner may act as their own contractor on their own primary residence by
signing an owner-builder affidavit at application. The exemption does not apply
to: work on properties intended for sale or rent within one year, commercial
buildings, or electrical service panel work — panel work always requires a
C-10 licensed electrician (see the Electrical Permit Standards).

## Verification and Penalties

License status is verified at application and again at permit issuance.
Performing permitted work with a lapsed or suspended license is a violation:
the County issues a stop-work order, and the permit fee for any corrective
permit is doubled. Repeat violations are referred to the state licensing board.
