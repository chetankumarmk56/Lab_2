# Permit Fees & Payment Policy

## Fee Schedule

Base permit fees are set annually by the Board of Supervisors. Current base
fees are:

- Residential building permit: $120
- Commercial building permit: $340
- Electrical permit (standalone): $85
- Plumbing permit (standalone): $85
- Mechanical/HVAC permit (standalone): $95

Plan review is charged at 65% of the base permit fee and is collected at
submission. Valuation-based surcharges apply to projects over $50,000 of
declared construction value, at $4 per additional $1,000 of value.

## When Fees Are Due

The plan review fee is due when the application is submitted. The remaining
permit fee is due before the permit is issued. A permit is not issued, and no
work may begin, until all fees are paid in full.

## Payment Methods

Fees may be paid online by card or ACH transfer, or at the counter by card,
check, or money order. Cash is accepted only at the counter and only for
amounts under $500.

## Refunds

If an application is withdrawn before plan review begins, 80% of fees paid are
refunded. Once plan review has started, the plan review fee is non-refundable.
No refunds are issued after a permit has been issued, except when the County
issued the permit in error.

## Reinspection Fee

If an inspection fails, or an inspector arrives and the work is not ready or
accessible, a reinspection fee of $75 is charged. The reinspection fee must be
paid before the reinspection is scheduled.

## Fee Waivers and Reductions

Registered nonprofit organizations building community facilities qualify for a
50% reduction of base permit fees. A financial hardship waiver may be requested
for owner-occupied residential repairs required by a code enforcement action;
hardship waivers are decided by the Building Official within 10 business days.
